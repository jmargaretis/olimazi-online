# Notes

I love desserts, but making them myself gives me more control over sugar and ingredients while teaching me the techniques behind them. Bougatsa is part of a larger goal: understand the three primary custard styles well enough to make them deliberately rather than simply follow instructions.

Recipes are also a way to share something with my family. I tried this again to maintain the slightly lighter filling, but did add a quick syrup similar to galaktoboureko to set it off more.

Galaktoboureko is King — next: a syrup variation closer to galaktoboureko, a small custard-learning series, and draft posts.
