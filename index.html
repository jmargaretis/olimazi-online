Previous work · brand build

Method Effects

A first-to-market automotive aero parts brand, designed and made in the U.S. A self-marketing product that held ~50% margins and earned real brand equity, but could not scale on a cash basis.

Period 2017–2024
Built Brand · product · suppliers · commerce · logistics
Reach Domestic and international direct-to-consumer sales
Process notes and lessons
  • Scope: fitment constraints, OEM mounting points, custom tooling and packaging, ecommerce, customer support, logistics, and content for Facebook, Instagram, and enthusiast forums.
  • Used vehicle scanning and professional CAD support to turn the initial idea into manufacturable geometry.
  • Designed around fitment, visibility, minimal modification, and an OEM-like appearance.
  • Retooled an expensive aluminum buck after rejecting a gap in the RS3 design; later learned MDF could have reduced that cost.
  • Custom boxes and shipping processes for a large product moving domestically and internationally. Duties, customs, and oversized shipping charges were constraints.
  • Faced pandemic-era plastic costs and overseas copying of product designs and images.

Learning in public

notes stapled to the wall, not a syllabus

I reached for tools first. Context came later.

Framed from Austin Marchese

NO. 1Chasing every new tool is window-shopping — pick one hub and plug everything into it.
NO. 2Nothing is hands-off from day one — you earn automation by working one system in absurd detail.
NO. 3More agents is more output, not more value — measure impact, not volume.
NO. 4A hundred downloaded skills is testing debt — building one against your own context is the point.
NO. 5There is no autopilot income — AI fulfills the work; it does not market it.
Context

Something I want to build on

A rental-property filing problem, a product process, or an interest I can judge for myself gives the AI work real context.

Constraints

Work around the gaps

Gemini in November 2025 wasn't sufficient, then came Claude in April 2026 — YouTube and X, Claude Code planning, and practical workarounds to fill gaps in technical background.

Handoff

Plan, execute, review

Planned in Claude Code, executed in Codex, reviewed in Claude Code. Around it: MCP setups like Zapier, Outlook workarounds, intake flows, and early Facebook and Instagram publishing systems.

Record

Keep the missing context

A roughly weekly record of what worked, what did not, and details tutorials often leave out — without pretending the learning is finished.
