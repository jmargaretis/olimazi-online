Method Effects

Method Effects was a ground-up, organically grown and first to market automotive aero parts company that facilitated a need for creativity and creation. It was a self marketing (pull) product that emphasized quality and control. The product was designed and manufactured in the U.S. while still realizing ~50% margins. Although unable to scale on a Cash-Basis, the brand earned high marks and a respected brand equity.

The work included fitment constraints, OEM mounting points, custom tooling and packaging, ecommerce, customer support, logistics, and content for Facebook, Instagram, and enthusiast forums.

Period2017–2023
BuiltBrand · product · suppliers · commerce · logistics
ReachDomestic and international direct-to-consumer sales
Process notes and lessons
  • Used vehicle scanning and professional CAD support to turn the initial idea into manufacturable geometry.
  • Designed around fitment, visibility, minimal modification, and an OEM-like appearance.
  • Retooled an expensive aluminum buck after rejecting a gap in the RS3 design; later learned MDF could have reduced that cost.
  • Custom boxes and shipping processes for a large product moving domestically and internationally. Duties, customs, and oversized shipping charges were constraints.
  • Faced pandemic-era plastic costs and overseas copying of product designs and images.

Learning in public02 / 03

I reached for tools first.
Context came later.

  • Chasing every new tool is window-shopping — pick one hub and plug everything into it.
  • Nothing is hands-off from day one — you earn automation by working one system in absurd detail.
  • More agents is more output, not more value — measure impact, not volume.
  • A hundred downloaded skills is testing debt — building one against your own context is the point.
  • There is no autopilot income — AI fulfills the work; it does not market it.

Framed from Austin Marchese ↗

Recipe experiment

Bougatsa — Greek custard pie

I love desserts, but making them myself gives me more control over sugar and ingredients while teaching me the techniques behind them. Bougatsa is part of a larger goal: understand the three primary custard styles well enough to make them deliberately rather than simply follow instructions.

Why it belongs here

Recipes are also a way to share something with family. They make a useful test for saving the original source, recording substitutions, drafting a post, and keeping the personal reason intact instead of producing generic food content.
